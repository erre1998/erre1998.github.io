                <div class="project-description">

                    <p>
                        <em>ToolingDrama</em> develops and consolidates digital tools for computational drama analysis for use in university teaching. Building on existing applications and software packages such as Katharsis and DramaAnalysis, the project evaluates their accessibility and suitability together with students, identifies technical and didactic barriers, and adapts selected tools for more sustainable and low-threshold use. Particular attention is given to portability, usability across operating systems, integration into humanities teaching, and the connection between digital methods and subject-specific research questions. The resulting tools and documentation are intended for continued use in teaching and independent student research and will be made openly available for reuse.
                    </p>

                </div>

                        <em>Digital Humanities im Fokus: Methoden, Anwendungen und Perspektiven</em>,
                        which brings together scholars from different areas of the Digital Humanities
                        and related disciplines to present current methods, research approaches,
                        infrastructures, and applications. By connecting research and teaching,
                        the project fosters interdisciplinary exchange, introduces students and
                        researchers to current developments in the field, and contributes to the
                        visibility and institutionalization of Digital Humanities at the University
                        of Rostock.
